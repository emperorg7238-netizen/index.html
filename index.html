For Fathia, My Agbeke

A letter from my heart to yours

My dearest Fathia, my Agbeke,

Sometimes I try to find the perfect words for what you mean to me, and I realize that words can only go so far. Still, I want to try, because you deserve to know just how deeply you have touched my heart.

You came into my life and somehow made ordinary moments feel softer, warmer, and more beautiful. Your presence has become one of those little things I look forward to, and your smile has a way of making my heart forget whatever was weighing on it.

I don't love you because everything is always perfect. I love you because even in the imperfect moments, I still choose you. I want to understand you, support you, laugh with you, grow with you, and be someone whose presence makes life feel a little lighter.

Fathia, I hope you never doubt how special you are to me. You deserve a love that feels safe, genuine, patient, and intentional. And if you'll let me, I want to keep showing you that my feelings aren't just words—they're something I want to prove through the way I treat you.

And since your special day is getting closer, let me say it before everyone else gets the chance: Happy birthday in advance, my love. 🎂❤️ I pray this new chapter brings you peace, beautiful surprises, answered prayers, and so many reasons to smile. I hope you become even more of the woman you dream of becoming, and I hope I get to witness many of those beautiful moments beside you.

No matter how many birthdays come and go, I hope you always remember this one thing: somewhere in this world, there is an Olamide who is incredibly grateful that Fathia exists.

Thank you for being you. Thank you for letting me know your heart. And thank you for giving my heart somewhere beautiful to belong.

Forever with a smile when I think of you,
Olamide ❤️
